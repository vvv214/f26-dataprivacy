## Course-specific policies

### Collaboration

- Labs, quizzes, and Canvas exit surveys are individual work.
- Projects may be completed individually or in teams of up to 2.
- High-level discussion with classmates is fine, but do not share lab code,
  filled-in tables, or polished written answers.
- Every submitted assignment should clearly list permitted collaborators.

### Use of GenAI

Generative AI tools may be used for brainstorming, debugging, or polishing, but they do not replace understanding.

- You remain responsible for correctness, citations, privacy, and policy compliance.
- Do not paste private course materials or sensitive data into external tools unless explicitly permitted.
- Quizzes must be completed without GenAI.

### Quizzes

- Both quizzes are completed during class, on paper, and individually.
- Quizzes are closed book and closed notes. Computers, phones, AI tools, and
  other electronic aids may not be used.
- Each quiz includes foundation and application questions plus longer
  certify-or-refute reasoning questions. An instructor-supplied formula sheet
  and a practice set will be provided before each quiz.
- Coverage and any other permitted basic supplies will be announced before each
  quiz.
- An approved absence receives an equivalent makeup arrangement. A makeup may
  use different examples while assessing the same skills.

### Exit surveys

- Short individual exit surveys submitted through Canvas contribute 10% of the
  course grade.
- An exit survey may ask for a conclusion, a remaining question, or an
  interpretation of a small result.
- Exit surveys receive credit for timely, good-faith completion.
- Contact the instructor about an extended absence.

### Late work

- Each student has 3 late days for the semester, with at most 2 on any one
  assignment.
- Late days can be used on any lab or project submission, but not on in-class
  activities.
- If you have a documented conflict or a serious issue, contact the instructor as early as possible.

### Regrades

- Regrade requests must be submitted within 7 days after a grade is released and
  may result in review of the entire submission.
- The result of a regrade request for a project team submission applies to the
  full team.

### Project contributions

- Teams must include a short contribution statement for every project milestone.
- Shared grades are the default, but substantial contribution imbalances may lead to adjustments.

## University policies and resources

The links below point to current UVA sources. If a summary on this page
conflicts with University policy, the official policy controls.

### Academic integrity

Students are subject to UVA's [Honor Code](https://honor.virginia.edu/course/honor-code)
and official guidance on [academic fraud](https://honor.virginia.edu/academic-fraud).
The course-specific collaboration and GenAI rules above explain how those
expectations apply to this course. Ask before submitting if a permitted boundary
is unclear.

### Academic accommodations

Section 6 of UVA policy
[PROV-008: Teaching Courses for Academic Credit](https://uvapolicy.virginia.edu/policy/PROV-008)
governs academic accommodations for disability, pregnancy, and religion.

- For disability-related accommodations, contact the
  [Student Disability Access Center](https://sdac.studenthealth.virginia.edu/).
- For pregnancy-related accommodations, see EOCR's
  [Student Pregnancy Accommodations](https://eocr.virginia.edu/accommodations/pregnancy-accommodations/student-pregnancy-accommodations).
- For religious accommodations, see EOCR's
  [Student Religious Accommodations](https://eocr.virginia.edu/student-religious-accommodations).

Students should contact the instructor as early as possible so appropriate
accommodations can be arranged and implemented in the course.

### Discrimination, harassment, retaliation, and sexual misconduct

The applicable University policies are
[HRM-009](https://uvapolicy.virginia.edu/policy/HRM-009),
[HRM-010](https://uvapolicy.virginia.edu/policy/HRM-010), and
[HRM-041](https://uvapolicy.virginia.edu/policy/HRM-041). The instructor and any
TAs are Responsible Employees under
[HRM-040](https://uvapolicy.virginia.edu/policy/HRM-040) and cannot promise
confidentiality for disclosures that University policy requires them to report.

For current reporting options, confidential resources, and an explanation of
these obligations, see the official [EOCR syllabus statement](https://eocr.virginia.edu/syllabus)
and [Just Report It](https://justreportit.virginia.edu/).

## Career-related travel

Your career development path may take you beyond UVA for an internship interview
or a technical conference. If you notify the instructor at least one week in
advance of such an event, you may choose either:

1. completing assignments remotely; or
2. delaying assignment, exam, or quiz dates until at least 48 hours after your
   return.

Extensions will not normally be granted for a project team activity when only
one member is traveling, and no delay will extend beyond the last day of finals
for the semester.
